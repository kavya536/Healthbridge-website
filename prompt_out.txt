4.6 Admin
The most underrated build. The Admin dashboard is what makes the platform survive its first year. Build this with the same care as the patient app.
Admin controls
•	Approvals — doctor licenses, supplier KYC, hospital onboarding queue with SLA tracking.
•	Disputes — patient complaints, refund requests, doctor-pharmacy substitution conflicts, evidence-based resolution workflow.
•	Verification — NMC scrape, Drug License Form 20/21 verification, GST verification, bank account penny-drop.
•	Payouts — doctor and supplier settlement, hold-and-release based on dispute status, TDS deduction.
•	Moderation — content flags, reported reviews, advisory board case queue.
•	Analytics — funnel by city, specialty, role; cohort retention; LTV by acquisition channel.
•	Commissions — dynamic commission rules by city, specialty, and role tier.
•	Compliance — audit logs (7-year retention for prescriptions, 3-year for everything else), DPDP Act consent ledger, breach notification workflow.
